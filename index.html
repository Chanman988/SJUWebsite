1st Gavin Dempsey

You know who Tweek is come on.

2nd Xavier

Xavier’s Mario makes every hit count. Get caught in the wrong spot and one clean combo can send your stock flying.

3rd Ralph

Ralph’s Yoshi is tough to pin down. He’s always moving, and once he starts a string, it can feel like there’s no getting off the ride.

4th Noodl

Noodl makes DK look way faster than a giant ape has any right to be. Blink at the wrong time and you’re eating a punish that gets the whole crowd reacting.

5th Vince

Vince plays Roy like he’s always one step from starting a brawl. Get caught near the hilt and you’ll feel every bit of that sword.

6th TJ13

TJ13 keeps Sonic moving and makes you work for every clean hit. Look away for a second and he’s already found a way in—or out.

7th LittleMacMain

LittleMacMain makes Min Min’s reach a problem from the very first second. Getting close is the hard part; staying there is even harder.

8th Grey

Grey makes you deal with the whole screen. Between Mii Gunner’s shots and traps, rushing in is never as easy as it looks.

9th Antonio

Antonio brings Sora’s big swings and aerial combos to life. Give him one opening and you might be taking a tour of the sky.

10th Zach

Zach’s R.O.B. is a menace with a gyro on the field. Ignore it and you’re in trouble; focus on it and Zach gets room to work.
